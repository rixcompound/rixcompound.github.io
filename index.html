    About Rix Compound

    Where Every Ride Becomes an Adventure! 🏁🔥

    Nestled in the heart of the Cape Winelands, Rix Compound is a private pit bike and junior MX track designed for riders of all ages and skill levels. Whether you’re hopping on a bike for the first time or you’re a seasoned rider craving dust and adrenaline, our track offers the perfect blend of fun, excitement, and challenge.

    We host open days, weekend rides, and Sunday Funday sessions — grab your bike, bring your friends, and enjoy the thrill of the dirt. Follow our page for track updates, event announcements, and ride highlights. 🏍️🔥

    The Experience 🤝

    Our purpose-built compound is designed for pure enjoyment and group excitement. Perfect for:

    • 🎉 Birthday Parties packed with energy and laughter
    • 🤝 Team-Building Events that inspire competition and camaraderie
    • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Group Outings for both kids and adults

    We supply all the equipment — you just bring the energy. Our track is compact, controlled, and family-friendly, making it the ideal environment for kids learning to ride while adults enjoy the thrill. Big bikes only allowed on atv track, any mini moto/pit bike allowed on small track.

    We tailor our rental packages to suit any group size or occasion, providing an outdoor experience you’ll want to repeat.
